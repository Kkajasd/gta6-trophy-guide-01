Roadmap to the platinum

Four stages, in order. Stage 1 is the story — play it through. Stage 2 is the missable moments — do them when they come up, before the finale. Stages 3 and 4 are the open world, done any time after the story.

Missable trophies — do these first

Four trophies can be missed. Each needs a moment that may not come back: a partner beside you, a one-time airport exit, or a story choice. Do them the moment the chance appears — after the finale, the chance may be gone.
