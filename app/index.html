    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official descriptions: story first, then activities and skills, then collectibles and exploration, then cleanup. Earn the story trophies in chapter order; the missable ones are flagged on the Missables tab — get them before the story moves on.

    Missable trophies

    These four are flagged missable from their official descriptions — each needs something that may not be available later (a story choice, or your partner). Flags are built from the descriptions, not yet verified in the game: earn them when the chance comes, before the final mission.

      How a trophy gets flagged: a trophy is missable when its official description needs a one-time story choice, a partner who may not be around later, or a single moment at a single place. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam.
