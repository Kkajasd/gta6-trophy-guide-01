Leonida Platinum — an offline web app that guides and tracks all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: the official list with its names, descriptions and grades, a checklist saved in your browser with export and import, filters by kind, a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until earned, a four-stage roadmap to the platinum, the missable trophies flagged, and a guide to each trophy in our own words that cites its sources. No network requests, no account, no ads.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — tick trophies off as you earn them, open any trophy for its guide and sources, and follow the Roadmap tab from the story to the platinum.
